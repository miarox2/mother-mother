Personalized Infant & Toddler Sleep Consulting

When nights feel long and naps are unpredictable, it can be hard to know what to try next. I’m Mia, a certified infant sleep consultant and mom of three. I help families make sense of their little one’s sleep and find a practical path toward more rest.

Let’s Connect

A plan that fits your real life

Your child’s sleep patterns, developmental needs, and your family’s routines all matter. We’ll look at them together and build an approach around your values and comfort level. My guidance is evidence-informed and flexible, with encouragement and adjustments along the way. The goal is to help you understand your options and feel confident making decisions for your family.

What support can include

  • A thoughtful assessment of your child’s sleep patterns and developmental needs.
  • A personalized sleep plan that considers your routines, values, and lifestyle.
  • Practical guidance for building sustainable sleep habits.
  • Education about infant and toddler sleep development.
  • Ongoing encouragement, troubleshooting, and adjustments as needed.

Is this support right for your family?

If bedtime takes longer than you’d like, night wakings leave everyone exhausted, or naps and sleep transitions feel confusing, we can explore what’s going on together. Support is individualized for infants and toddlers, with room for your questions and the realities of family life.

Virtual support, wherever you are

I offer virtual infant and toddler sleep consulting to families worldwide. You can work with me from Long Beach, Los Angeles, Orange County, or wherever your family calls home.

Kind words from families

Your questions, answered

Do we need to live near you?

No. Sleep consulting is available virtually to families worldwide. Share your location and time zone when you reach out so we can discuss working together.

Will the plan account for our family’s routine?

Yes. Your child’s needs, your daily rhythm, and your family’s values help shape the plan. We can troubleshoot and make adjustments as needed.

How do I learn about packages and pricing?

Send an inquiry with your child’s age, the sleep challenges you’re experiencing, and what you’re hoping for. We can discuss the current options, pricing, and support that fits your family.
