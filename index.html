Welcome to Mother Mother

Rooted in the belief that mothers deserve to be mothered, Mother Mother offers thoughtful, personalized care for the whole family through pregnancy, birth, postpartum, and beyond.

Through birth and postpartum doula support, sleep consulting, and yoga, I help families reclaim rest and joy by nurturing the mind, body, and soul.

Whether you’re welcoming your first baby or growing your family, I’m here to walk alongside you through all the beautiful, messy, and wonderfully unpredictable moments of parenthood.

Explore my offerings or reach out to connect. I’d love to hear from you!

Hi, I’m Mia!

I’m a birth and postpartum doula, certified infant sleep consultant, yoga teacher, and mom of three.

After having three babies in three years (yes, three!), I know firsthand just how beautiful, exhausting, hilarious, and completely overwhelming those early years can be. Through it all, I developed a deep appreciation for just how sacred birth and postpartum truly are, and how meaningful it is to feel cared for during these seasons of life. Somewhere along the way, I found myself drawn to supporting other families through these incredible transitions.

Mother Mother was born from a simple belief: mothers deserve to be mothered, too. And while that’s at the heart of what I do, I believe that caring for parents means caring for the whole family.

I bring a peaceful, steady presence to my work, but I also love good conversation, plenty of laughter, and cheering families on through all the little victories along the way. Whether we’re preparing for birth, navigating life with a newborn, figuring out sleep, or moving together through yoga, my hope is that you feel as comfortable with me as you would with a friend — while knowing you have someone knowledgeable and dependable in your corner.

Before becoming a doula, I studied musical theater at UCLA and spent years performing. These days, I still love music, movement, cooking, and finding little ways to make everyday life feel special.

You aren’t meant to do this alone. I’d love to be part of your village.

Birth Doula Support

Birth is a deeply personal experience, and every family deserves to feel informed, supported, and cared for along the way.

As your birth doula, I’m here to offer emotional, physical, and informational support throughout pregnancy, labor, and birth. From answering questions and preparing for the big day to offering comfort, reassurance, and encouragement through labor, my role is to help you feel confident, cared for, and supported every step of the way.

I’m also here for your partner! Birth is a journey for the whole family, and I love helping partners feel involved, prepared, and confident in their role. Think of me as an extra set of hands, a steady presence, and one of your biggest cheerleaders as you welcome your baby into the world.

Based in Los Alamitos, I offer in-person birth doula support for families in Long Beach, Greater Los Angeles, and Orange County.

What You Can Expect:

  • Personalized prenatal support to explore your birth preferences, questions, and hopes
  • Education and resources to help you approach birth with confidence
  • Continuous emotional and physical support throughout labor and birth
  • Comfort measures, breathing techniques, positioning, and movement to support labor
  • Guidance and encouragement for partners so they feel involved and supported
  • Support with communication and informed decision-making throughout your birth experience
  • A calm, reassuring presence that honors your choices and helps you feel heard
  • Two postpartum visits to reflect on your birth experience, answer questions, and support your family’s transition into life with a new baby

Postpartum Doula Support

Welcoming a new baby brings incredible joy — and a whole lot of change! Those first weeks can be beautiful, exhausting, emotional, and sometimes all of the above before breakfast.

As your postpartum doula, I’m here to make life a little easier. Whether you need help figuring out feeding, someone to hold the baby while you shower, a nourishing meal, or just a little reassurance that you’re doing a wonderful job, I’m here for all of it.

My goal is to care for the whole family, helping you find your rhythm, feel more confident, and actually enjoy those sweet (and sometimes chaotic!) early days together.

I offer in-person postpartum doula care in Long Beach, Greater Los Angeles, and Orange County, with support shaped around your family’s needs.

What You Can Expect:

  • Compassionate, hands-on newborn care tailored to your family’s needs
  • Gentle guidance with feeding, soothing, and newborn routines
  • Emotional support, encouragement, and a listening ear as you navigate early parenthood
  • Practical assistance with light household tasks, meal preparation, and sibling care
  • Education and personalized guidance to help you feel confident caring for your baby
  • Support and encouragement for partners as they adjust to their new roles
  • Thoughtful resources and referrals to support your family’s well-being
  • A calm, loving presence (and an extra set of hands!) dedicated to caring for you and your family

Infant & Toddler Sleep Consulting

Sleep is essential for the whole family’s well-being, but sometimes getting everyone to actually sleep feels like an impossible task.

If bedtime has become a marathon, naps are anyone’s guess, or you can’t remember the last time you slept through the night, you’re in the right place.

I work with families to better understand their little one’s sleep needs and create personalized, realistic plans that support healthy, sustainable sleep habits. My approach combines evidence-informed guidance with empathy, flexibility, and plenty of encouragement. No two babies are the same, and no two families are either!

Together, we’ll find an approach that feels right for your family so everyone can get a little more of the rest they deserve.

Infant and toddler sleep consulting is available virtually to families worldwide, including those here in Long Beach, Los Angeles, and Orange County.

What You Can Expect:

  • A thoughtful assessment of your child’s unique sleep patterns and developmental needs
  • A customized, practical sleep plan that considers your family’s routines, values, and lifestyle
  • Clear, supportive guidance for building healthy, sustainable sleep habits
  • Education about infant and toddler sleep development
  • Ongoing encouragement, troubleshooting, and adjustments as needed
  • A flexible, compassionate approach that empowers you to make decisions with confidence

Yoga for Every Body

Yoga is for every body — whether you’re pregnant, postpartum, or simply looking for a little time to reconnect with yourself.

I believe yoga should feel welcoming, approachable, and even a little playful. It’s not about perfect poses or touching your toes (although that’s fun, too!). It’s about moving your body, finding your breath, and creating a little space for yourself in the middle of everyday life.

I offer personalized yoga sessions that meet you exactly where you are. From prenatal and postpartum practices to yoga for strength, flexibility, and mindfulness, we’ll move in a way that feels good for your body and the season of life you’re in.

Personalized, in-person yoga sessions are available throughout Greater Los Angeles and Orange County.

What You Can Expect:

  • Personalized sessions tailored to your goals, experience, and physical needs
  • Supportive guidance through prenatal, postpartum, or general yoga practices
  • An emphasis on mindful breathing, body awareness, strength, and flexibility
  • Thoughtful modifications for different levels and abilities
  • Space to reconnect with your body, release tension, and cultivate calm
  • Simple tools to bring mindfulness and movement into your everyday life
